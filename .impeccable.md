# Design Context — LokFeel (Impeccable)

## Target audience
Teams, professional communities and industry-network operators with real members, owners and collaboration tasks. B2B/professional readers who evaluate tools by trust and verifiability, not by hype.

## Use cases
- Record collaboration intent → send proposals → get explicit consent → let agents act under approval → verify outcomes (three levels: self-reported / counterparty-confirmed / externally-verified).
- Organization admins manage members, roles, audit, quotas.

## Brand personality / tone
- "Structured over conversational. Consent is a first-class object. Recoverable by design. Open at the edges."
- Trustworthy, precise, calm-professional. Evidence over adjectives. No dark-pattern urgency, no AI-slop aesthetics.
- The memorable point: verification levels and consent gates rendered as tangible, readable UI objects — proof you can see.

## Constraints
- Next.js App Router, React 19, Tailwind; public pages are English-first; WCAG 2.2 AA target (contrast ≥4.5:1 body, ≥3:1 large text/borders); keyboard reachable; prefers-reduced-motion respected.
- Reuse the existing public-site design language (tokens/fonts from current (public) pages); do not invent a second design system.
- Honest copy only: claims must map to `docs/release/lokfeel-final-20261110/01_CONTENT_AND_CLAIMS.csv`.
